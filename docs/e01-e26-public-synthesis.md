This is a non-interventional interpretation of the frozen E01–E26 record. The underlying primary records remain privately retained; stable evidence IDs resolve through the public evidence catalog and the research owner's private hash/selector registry. This graph and dataset are small derivatives, not substitutes for primary evidence. No new benchmark panel, optimization, retraining, or E27 was performed to produce this synthesis.

The series closes after E26 because the accepted evidence no longer uniquely nominates a next intervention. Further experimentation requires a new independently motivated hypothesis rather than selection of the largest timer. **Economic exhaustion is UNKNOWN. Future work is not blocked.** The historical report retained “INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE TO DECIDE” about continuation versus conclusion. The subsequent user-directed decision closes this series at that evidence boundary while economic exhaustion remains UNKNOWN; it does not revise the experimental classification.

## Reading the record

E01–E03 establish bounded optimizer, reverse-mode and tensor-identity reference boundaries. E04–E26 then investigate selective execution and the surrounding state-management, representation and validation work. The accompanying dataset retains all 26 experiment-local classifications and comparison boundaries. Letter labels are local to their original protocols. They cannot be compared as if “A” or “B” were universal scientific scores. [EV-E01-REPORT–EV-E26-REPORT]

The strongest positive evidence is conditional: authenticated change receipts enabled warm frozen-output reuse; compact frontiers established selected active host-output wins; some gains survived persistent backend publication; bounded incremental request/plan cases created further operating regions. These mechanisms were tested with their own FULL controls. Their timings are not pooled or treated as successive samples of one unchanged implementation. [EV-E07-REPORT–EV-E12-REPORT]

The strongest negative evidence is equally useful: arithmetic savings alone did not pay for discovery, dense materialization, overlay management or per-operation mediation. Later exact-representation improvements often benefited both FULL and differential paths. Reuse validation, first admission and recurring output integrity remained substantial. A narrow original incremental-plan win did not reproduce in later retained-control regressions; that later observation does not erase the original measurement. [EV-E04-REPORT–EV-E06-REPORT, EV-E11-REPORT–EV-E19-REPORT]

## Six kinds of persistence

| Kind | Empirical support | Boundary |

| Semantic | No consequential numerical change and native occurrence/history distinctions can be represented explicitly | Equal final values do not erase intervening generations, dependency changes or edit history [EV-E07-REPORT, EV-E12-REPORT] |
| Relational | Current coordinate/occurrence-to-backing associations can persist across request reordering | E21 reuse requires exact eligibility; consequential source changes invalidate the tested relation reuse [EV-E21-REPORT] |
| Representation | Canonical immutable components can be reused when their exact representation semantics remain unchanged | Whole ordered descriptors can change despite persistent members; exact bytes are not current authority [EV-E19-REPORT, EV-E22-REPORT] |
| Construction | Current construction can produce a checked one-shot slot/span witness | E23/E24 establish construction-scoped evidence, not indefinitely persistent or cross-generation witnesses [EV-E23-REPORT, EV-E24-REPORT] |

## Structural and authority decay

A defensible descriptive candidate is `D_s(P, k | fixture)`: the fraction of observed pairs separated by k generations satisfying a *named* structural predicate P. The predicate, sampled pair set and denominator must be explicit. For E26, the retained summary supports only the listed adjacent-transition (`k = 1`) ordered and multiset fractions, plus a differently weighted common-occurrence agreement ratio. The ratios must not be merged into a single structural score. The derived dataset simply retains their exact counts. It does not construct unrecorded distant pairs. [EV-E26-CONTINUITY]

`D_a(k)` would require an explicit authority applicability predicate, complete authority-state observations or controlled validity/revocation tests across those distances, and an adequate sampled denominator. That longitudinal measurement does not exist here. Tested stale/current checks and categorical eligibility boundaries support distinguishing structure from authority, but not estimating authority survival by time or distance. E25 records unknown private-owner epochs; E26 does not test a cross-generation provenance-result cache. [EV-E25-REPORT, EV-S26P]

**Result: structural persistence has bounded predicate-specific empirical support; authority applicability is a distinct categorical constraint. Dual smooth decay, exponential decay, a universal half-life, and a scalar structural/authority decay law are unsupported.** E23's one-shot construction evidence adds a lifecycle class, not another measured generational decay curve.

## Reuse tension

The record supports a qualitative tension: persistent structure can coexist with expensive current applicability checks, representation work and output integrity. A normalized cross-experiment `T(v)` would require common definitions of avoided work, management costs, bootstrap amortization, output contracts and statistical units. These boundaries change across the sequence. No cross-experiment scalar `T(v)` is defined. [EV-E09-REPORT, EV-E14-REPORT, EV-E17-REPORT, EV-S26P]

Likewise, `T_A(v)` is **not numerically defined**. E26's broad timer includes projection/construction/equality/append and does not isolate removable authority work. `NetBenefit = AvoidedWork − ReuseManagementCost` is an accounting requirement, not an evaluated E26 metric: neither quantity is established for the hypothetical provenance-result cache. E17's observed hit integrity share and E26's observed loop share remain their own measured fractions, not converted into an invented reuse-tension score. [EV-S17, EV-S26P]

## Economic operating map

The JSON dataset is the operating-region map: each row carries its own comparison boundary, result and limitation. It deliberately contains no cross-study ranking by milliseconds.

- **Arithmetic or logical storage reduction without total gain:** E04–E06. Logical bytes copied/reused do not establish physical memory, bandwidth, energy or hardware savings.
- **New bounded differential wins against appropriate contemporaneous FULL controls:** E07's eligible frozen output, E08's selected warm host frontiers, E09's selected persistent sequences, E11's original fixed-request case, E12's request-only changes. E11's later regression and E12's joint-change loss remain explicit.
- **Structural or path improvement without a new historical region:** E10 and E13–E16; E21 relational reuse improves its Single path while its FULL counterpart and changed-source case lose.
- **Generic representation/admission improvement:** E17–E19 and E23. Both matched FULL and Single can benefit. E17's faster hits do not establish economical cumulative result reuse.
- **Measurement-only or non-reproduced end-to-end gain:** E20, E22, E24–E26. No speedup was tested in E26.

No sampled result establishes universal acceleration, an intrinsic lower bound, a hardware ΔPU or a model-quality improvement. The sequence's later clean surfaces still retain coarse instrumentation and the CPU `-Onone` testbed. Observer-level differences and two-process descriptive replication are not population confidence estimates.

## Fourteen longitudinal answers

1. **Most reliable persistence.** Within eligible reorder-only fixtures, relational membership/coordinate-to-backing associations are more stable than order-dependent representations. No ordering-independent claim is extended to consequential source changes. [EV-E21-REPORT, EV-E26-CONTINUITY]
2. **Repeated reconstruction.** Current requested order, compiled/current provenance vectors, representation assembly and first-admission products repeatedly remain fresh even when underlying relations persist. [EV-E21-REPORT–EV-E26-REPORT]
3. **Fresh authority.** Source, publication, index, plan, request and private result/context applicability remain current obligations at the relevant call/temporal boundary. Evidence of equality is not admission. [EV-E17-REPORT, EV-E21-REPORT, EV-E25-REPORT]
4. **Benefits to both paths.** Sealed contexts, single metadata serialization, exact component reuse and current construction evidence improve generic validation/representation mechanisms in matched FULL as well as Single. Their economic outcomes differ by experiment. [EV-E17-REPORT–EV-E19-REPORT, EV-E23-REPORT]
5. **Differential wins.** Authenticated change knowledge, compact active frontiers, sparse/local persistent publication, original incremental fixed plans and bounded request-only deltas establish specific operating regions. They do not license a general claim across consumers. [EV-E07-REPORT–EV-E12-REPORT]
6. **Management erasing arithmetic savings.** Discovery/dense realization, overlay lookup/metadata, per-operation mediation, bootstrap, full-vector reuse validation and first-result sealing each defeat apparently favorable local savings in their tested boundaries. [EV-E04-REPORT–EV-E06-REPORT, EV-E14-REPORT, EV-E16-REPORT, EV-E17-REPORT]
7. **Migration rather than disappearance.** Eliminating lineage walks exposes plan construction; local order maintenance exposes exact commitments; cheaper recurring commitments exposes bootstrap; cheaper hits exposes first admission; representation improvements expose distributed validation. Earlier costs remain where the later intervention does not cover them. [EV-E10-REPORT–EV-E26-REPORT]
8. **Consequential source change.** Correctness/fallback protections survive their tested changed-source cases; several economic advantages do not. E21 relation eligibility fails and E25/E26 index acquisition expands, while E26's fixed-width internal-provenance work remains broadly similar. This is not evidence of safe provenance-result-cache revocation, which was not implemented. [EV-E21-REPORT, EV-E25-REPORT, EV-S26E, EV-S26P]
9. **E26 addition.** Persistent associations do not imply persistent ordered products, reusable validation decisions, preserved authority or removable current-loop work. The six persistence categories therefore need separate evidence. [EV-E26-CONTINUITY, EV-S26P]
10. **Preserve validated structure?** Supported as a conditional design principle: preserve exact useful structure with explicit identity, applicability and invalidation contracts when its management costs are justified. It is neither a theorem that every validation can be cached nor a mandate to retain accidental instrumentation. [EV-E10-REPORT, EV-E17-REPORT, EV-E21-REPORT, EV-E24-REPORT]
11. **ΔPU implication.** Computing consequences of change requires more than sparse arithmetic: authority, dependency maintenance, persistent representation and consumer contracts affect the execution frontier and economics. The record motivates an execution-model research direction, not established dedicated hardware. [EV-E04-REPORT–EV-E26-REPORT]
12. **Architecture-independent conclusions.** The logical distinctions between value/history, relation/order, representation/applicability, and proposal/authority/commit are portable design lessons. Their empirical cost magnitudes are not architecture-independent. [EV-E07-REPORT, EV-E09-REPORT, EV-E12-REPORT, EV-E21-REPORT, EV-E26-REPORT]
13. **Testbed-specific conclusions.** Observed crossover cells, timer rankings, serialization/array/equality overhead, warm starts, MLX CPU primitives and `-Onone` execution can change on another compiler, backend or architecture. No general tensor-kernel or GPU speed claim follows. [EV-E08-REPORT–EV-E26-REPORT]
14. **UNKNOWN.** Pure CLEAN construction/comparison attribution; general validation-result/authority persistence; safe hypothetical cache revocation; cache-management cost/net benefit; physical savings; production scaling/model quality; exhaustive host trust; and economic exhaustion. E17 recurring output integrity remains unresolved. [EV-E01-REPORT–EV-E03-REPORT, EV-E17-REPORT, EV-E19-REPORT, EV-E26-REPORT]

## Research-integrity limits

Preserve strict positional Set FAIL separately from source-faithful multiset PASS. This is an audit-contract distinction, not a correction of raw order. Preserve observer perturbation, rejected attempts, and the bounded host-observation envelope. E26's approximately 80% instrumented mixed-loop concentration is not a pure CLEAN construction share. Repetition of a check is not evidence that its temporal authority obligation is redundant. [EV-E20-REPORT–EV-E26-REPORT]

The proper successor is a small foundation that makes state, proposed transition, verification and commit distinct. Future numerical, cloud and isolation adapters can be evaluated independently. None requires resolving every E26 unknown first. This successor recommendation is post-hoc architecture, not a retroactive experimental finding or authorization to implement later roadmap phases.
